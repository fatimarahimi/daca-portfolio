## About

I'm a product manager who started on the front line. Six years in technical support and onboarding at Linnworks and Malwarebytes taught me to find the real problem behind a request — and to explain complex things simply. Since then I've built products in IoT connectivity and cybersecurity SaaS, and I wanted to stop relying on others for the numbers behind my decisions. So I learned to pull them myself.

At 1oT, I worked with telecom partners like Elisa and Bite on a SIM and eSIM management platform, and led a 9-month billing migration from Braintree to Stripe for ~400 business customers — over 90% self-migrated, zero revenue lost. Through a data analytics programme I added SQL, Python and Power BI: I segmented 2,540 customers (18% of them brought in 42.8% of revenue) and built an automated pipeline processing 10,118 sales in 2.5 seconds.

I'm looking for a product role where data drives decisions — in SaaS, IoT or cybersecurity, in Tallinn or remote. My portfolio is at github.com/Fatimarahimi. Happy to connect and talk product, data or both.
