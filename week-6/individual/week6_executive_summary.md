# Tallinn Store: Executive Summary (2023–2024)

1. **Strength:** Summer is Tallinn's strongest season, peaking at €52K–55K, well above the €42K monthly average.
2. **Opportunity:** September drops to €32K–35K in both years, below the €42K average. A back-to-school or autumn campaign could fill the gap.
3. **Risk:** Sales drop to €30K–31K in Jan–Feb, less than half of December's €64K. Big swings make stock and staffing harder to plan.
4. **Recommendation:** Run a September back-to-school campaign and a January sale, then compare results with this year's €32K–35K and €30K–31K.
