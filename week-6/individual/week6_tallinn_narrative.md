# Tallinn Store: Data Story (2023–2024)

Tallinn is UrbanStyle's biggest store, bringing in €1.02M, or 38% of all sales in 2023–2024. Its 3.2K customers spend an average of €287 per order, and December sales grew 42% year on year. But sales follow a clear seasonal pattern, peaking in summer and dropping in September and January–February. We recommend a September back-to-school campaign and a January sale, then comparing results with 2023–2024.
