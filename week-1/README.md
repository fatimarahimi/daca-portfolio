# Week 1 — SQL Exploration

## What was the task?

Get familiar with SQL — writing queries and interpreting the results. Use this to explore the UrbanStyle sales and customer data, identify data quality issues, and present findings in a business-friendly format so a stakeholder (Toomas) could use them to make decisions.

## What did I do?

- Learned SQL basics at Shu level (copy-first, understand-after) — practiced `SELECT`, `FROM`, `WHERE`, `LIMIT`, `ORDER BY`, `AND`/`OR`, `COUNT`, and `COUNT(DISTINCT)`
- Ran these queries against the UrbanStyle sales and customer tables and interpreted the results
- Identified data quality issues: duplicate entries, customers sharing emails, inconsistent/misspelled city names, negative prices (refunds), missing customer IDs
- In group work, consolidated findings across the team into a presentation for the stakeholder to support a business decision
- Queries saved in `week1_sales_exploration.sql` and `week1_customers_exploration.sql`, screenshot in this folder

## What did I learn?

Data isn't just numbers — it tells a story once you know how to connect the dots. Building that analytical skill matters because clean, well-interpreted data gives stakeholders the clarity to make real business decisions.
